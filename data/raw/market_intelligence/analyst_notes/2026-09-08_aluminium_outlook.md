# Aluminium outlook - September 2026

LME aluminium cash averaged about 2,384 USD/t in July and has since climbed to around 2,667 USD/t month-to-date in September.

Drivers: smelter curtailments in Europe after an energy price spike, and lower Chinese exports.
Our base case is that prices stay elevated through Q4 2026, with upside risk if curtailments extend.

Implication for buyers: index-linked contracts with monthly review will pass the increase through from October. Check which customer agreements allow recovery and file claims on time.
